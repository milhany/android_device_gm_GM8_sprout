of all eight CPU cores being saturated. The log does not contain the main-thread
ANR stacks, so it cannot identify the exact Java/native method blocking the
receivers. Capture the corresponding /data/anr traces through a bugreport and
the package dexopt state before choosing another framework/ART change.

validate video decoding or camera frame pacing.
